# MediSync Dashboard Reveal V2

1920 × 1080, 30 fps, 300 frames. The previous composition remains available as DashboardRevealPrototype.

| Frames | Picture and Shotcraft mapping | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0–59 | Brand mark on warm white. ONE PLATFORM. and EVERY ROBOT. IN SYNC. assemble on two lines. Adapt the coordinated blur, lift and settle from BlurSlide and the spatial entry from AssembleThenTypeFlyin. | English VO enters at frame 18. Warm accent at frame 53. |
| 60–149 | The orange rule carries the eye upward as the title recedes. The English product capture rises and pulls back to front-on. Adapt CraneRiseReveal and MultiplaneReal for the changing horizon, perspective and depth. | Soft positioning tone at frame 144. |
| 150–239 | Crop real panel imagery to reveal the completion gauge, schedule and task progress, connection count, then battery trend. Adapt DeckDealFlyin's stagger and ContactShadowLift's shadow relationship. | A short digital accent at frame 216. |
| 240–299 | Real Dashboard panels settle into their original positions. Simulated Data sits at the edge and the orange Dispatch underline sets up the next scene. Hold a front-facing product frame. | Voice clears before the closing hold. |

The capture runs the product’s native English locale in a local browser. Frozen API fixtures provide 24 records (18 completed, 4 running, 2 pending), yielding the real Overview completion rate of 75%. Four different robots own the four running tasks. Already-connected fleet readings, scheduled tasks and route steps are presented in existing Overview panels. Nothing is sent to production, ROS, or the database.

The original brand mark comes from MediSyncBrand.vue. The white canvas, motion typography, transitional highlights and demo disclosure are film graphics. Voice is an offline English scratch read. The ambient track and cues are synthesized for this prototype.

Open DashboardRevealV2 in Remotion Studio. For capture instructions, use the local frontend command in QUICKSTART.md and run `node scripts/capture-dashboard-v2.mjs` from promo-video.
